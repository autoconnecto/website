## Entity (verbatim)

Autoconnecto (https://app.autoconnecto.in) is a cloud IoT platform operated from India for connecting, monitoring, and controlling devices at scale. Hosted multi-node deployment. Customer support by email (support@autoconnecto.in) and Telegram (@autoconnecto_community). It provides multi-tenant dashboards, real-time telemetry, a visual Rule Engine on ingest, profile Alarm Rules, Telegram ops-group + email alarms, Scheduler, Integration Hub, and MQTT/HTTPS/ESP32 connectivity. Documentation: https://docs.autoconnecto.in · Support: https://www.autoconnecto.in/support
